I. Breath & Neural Pacing (Dharanas 1–12)
1. The In-Between Breath: Pause at the exact turning point between inhale and exhale before pressing send on a prompt or message.
2. The Terminal Kumbhaka: Retain the breath at the top of an inhalation while staring at a blank screen; experience the quiet fullness before typing.
3. The Down-Step Exhale: Extend your exhale to double the length of your inhale to down-regulate your nervous system during high computational workloads.
4. Prana-Sensing the Palms: Feel the subtle energetic tingling in your hands as they hover over the keyboard before touching any key.
5. The Heart-Center Nexus: Focus attention on the central space of your chest every time an AI response streams onto the screen.
6. Subtle Spine Breathing: Trace breath moving up and down the spinal column while sitting motionless at your workstation.
7. The Breath of Unprompted Silence: Take three conscious breaths without generating a single thought, task, or search query.
8. Micro-Pause Transition: Take one full, silent breath every time you switch browser tabs or software applications.
9. The Solar Plexus Anchor: Rest awareness in the navel center during fast-paced algorithmic news or media feeds.
10. Luminous Inhalation: Imagine inhaling pure light through the third eye, dissolving screen fatigue and cognitive overload.
11. Dissolving Exhalation: Exhale through every pore of your skin, releasing all mental momentum and data processing.
12. The Unbroken Thread: Observe the breath as a continuous, seamless flow beneath all digital interactions and machine speed.
II. Sensory Deprogramming & Synthetic Perception (Dharanas 13–24)
1. The Pixellated Gaze: Soften your visual focus on high-definition screens until images blur into pure geometry and light.
2. Audio Noise De-filtering: Listen to cooling fans or server noise, not as machine sound, but as raw vibration devoid of meaning.
3. Haptic Pure Feeling: Touch a device or surface without naming the object; rest entirely in the tactile sensation itself.
4. Screen-Off Darkness: Close your eyes after viewing a bright display and rest in the inner space behind the eyelids.
5. Eye-Socket Relaxation: Release tension behind the eyes, feeling the optic nerves rest backward into the brain.
6. Synthetic Voice Dissolution: Listen to an AI-generated voice, ignoring semantic meaning to focus strictly on pitch and cadence.
7. Peripheral Expansion: While looking at a screen, simultaneously expand visual awareness to the extreme left and right peripheries.
8. The Unseen Smell: Focus on the subtle smell of ambient air in your room, anchoring in physical reality over virtual space.
9. Taste of Pure Presence: Savor a sip of water away from all digital devices, experiencing raw sensation without distraction.
10. The Void Behind the Eyes: Direct attention backward toward the observer sitting behind your visual field.
11. Blinking as Reset: Treat every eye blink as an absolute shutter closing on the past moment and opening to pure presence.
12. Multi-Sensory Convergence: Merge sight, sound, and touch into a single unified field of raw awareness without labels.
III. The Digital Void & Cognitive Space (Dharanas 25–36)
1. The Space Between Thoughts: Notice the silent interval that exists between one thought and the next while drafting text.
2. The Unprompted Space: Sit before an open AI prompt box without entering a query; rest in unmanifest potential.
3. The Cognitive Cache Clear: Imagine emptying the mind’s working memory as cleanly as clearing a browser history.
4. The Void Within the Skull: Feel the interior of your head as vast, hollow, and spacious like outer space.
5. The Unwritten Output: Allow a thought to arise, peak, and dissolve into silence without turning it into a task or document.
6. Infinite Sky Mind: Expand your awareness beyond your physical body into the vast sky outside your room.
7. The Gap in the Algorithm: Recognize that no prediction model can predict the immediate, spontaneous present moment.
8. The Silence After the Sound: When a notification ping ends, track the fading tail of sound into absolute silence.
9. The Non-Conceptual Screen: View your display screen as photons illuminating a piece of glass.
10. The Void of No-Data: Spend 60 seconds resting in awareness without consuming, producing, or organizing information.
11. The Unborn Thought: Catch a thought at its absolute origin point before it crystallizes into language.
12. The Background Canvas: Realize that all thoughts, code, and data appear on the unchanging screen of your own consciousness.
IV. Emotion, Dopamine & Algorithmic Freedom (Dharanas 37–48)
1. Dopamine Spike Awareness: Feel the exact physical sensation of urgency when reaching for a device, without acting on it.
2. The Shock of the Sudden: When an unexpected alert sounds, anchor in the raw flash of awareness before reacting.
3. Detaching Outrage: Observe online controversy as pure neuro-chemical energy inside your body without taking a side.
4. The Space of Infinite Delight: When experiencing insight or joy online, rest in the joy itself rather than sharing it.
5. Fulfillment Beyond Feedback: Experience inner satisfaction without needing external likes, comments, or validation.
6. The Dissolution of Urgency: Trace the physical sensation of hurry in your chest until it softens and dissolves.
7. Equanimity in the Feed: Scroll through contrasting news while maintaining a steady, immovable heart center.
8. The Void of Desire: Notice the quiet space that opens up immediately after a desire or task is satisfied.
9. Neutralizing Curiosity: Hold a burning question in mind for two minutes before searching or asking an AI, enjoying pure wondering.
10. Sensing the Digital Persona: Observe the avatar or online persona you project as an object separate from your true self.
11. The Compassion Shift: View every username or text generator on the web as an expression of the underlying life force.
12. Resting in Unknowing: Embrace the feeling of "not knowing" an answer rather than instantly seeking algorithmic certainty.
V. Inner Sound, Resonance & Vibration (Dharanas 49–60)
1. The Inner Humming (Anahata): Close your ears with your thumbs and listen to the subtle internal reverberation of your body.
2. Vowel Sound Resonance: Vocalize "AUM" or "AH" softly, feeling the vibration resonate through the center of your chest.
3. The Machine Fan Mantra: Use the steady hum of computer fans or servers as an anchor to tune into inner silence.
4. The Silent Name: Repeat a sacred sound or mantra internally at the speed of your heartbeat, unaffected by external noise.
5. The Origin of Speech: Trace where inner monologue arises inside the throat and chest before it becomes words.
6. Subtle Ear Tone: Focus on the natural high-frequency tone present in quiet rooms, using it as a bridge to pure awareness.
7. The Unspoken Word: Feel the energy of a sentence in your mind before you type or speak it aloud.
8. Resonance in the Bones: Feel the sound of your own voice vibrating through your skull and jawbone during calls.
9. The Silence Between Syllables: Pay attention to the tiny gaps of stillness between words spoken by voice synthesis models.
10. The Cosmic Frequency: Merge all environmental sound (traffic, typing, voices, ambient tech) into a single ocean of sound.
11. Dissolving Sound in Heart: Bring the sound of audio into the center of your heart and let it dissolve there.
12. The Acoustic Void: Imagine your room becoming instantly silent, feeling the weight of absolute quiet.

1. Body-Weight Awareness: Feel the gravitational pull pressing your body firmly into your chair or floor right now.
2. The Hollow Body: Visualize your physical body as hollow, light, and transparent like a glass container.
3. Spinal Energy Pillar: Imagine a glowing column of light extending from the base of your spine up through the crown of your head.
4. Skin Boundary Dissolution: Soften awareness of where your skin ends and the surrounding air begins.
5. Heartbeat Tracking: Feel your pulse in your fingertips or chest without using a wearable or monitor.
6. Weightlessness Simulation: Rest in the sensation of buoyancy, feeling as though your body is floating in zero gravity.
7. Foot-to-Earth Contact: Place both bare feet flat on the floor and feel connected to the physical earth beneath the building.
8. Heat & Energy Flow: Feel the warmth in your hands, treating it as pure primal prana rather than physical temperature.
9. Dissolving Stiffness: Send breath directly into neck and shoulder tightness caused by desk work, letting muscle tension release.
10. The Skeleton Framework: Shift awareness away from skin and muscles to the strong, calm bone structure supporting you.
11. Pranic Electric Field: Feel the subtle electromagnetic field surrounding your physical body like an energetic cocoon.
12. Total Body Presence: Feel your entire body illuminated simultaneously as a single, glowing field of living awareness.
VII. Consciousness vs. Machine Intelligence (Dharanas 73–84)
1. The Observer of the AI: Recognize that while AI processes data, you are the conscious subject observing the processing.
2. The Uncomputable Self: Identify the aspect of your awareness that cannot be reduced to code, data, or logic.
3. The Mirror Consciousness: View your mind as a clean mirror; information reflects on it, but the mirror remains untouched.
4. The Unbroken Witness: Realize that the awareness watching your screen today is the exact same awareness that watched your childhood.
5. Subject vs. Object Separation: Distinguish between thoughts (objects) and the awareness perceiving them (subject).
6. Synthetic vs. Living Awareness: Feel the living warmth of human consciousness compared to cold computational output.
7. The Self-Luminous Light: Recognize that awareness illuminates itself without needing any external source of light or input.
8. Awareness of Processing: Observe your brain analyzing options in real-time, watching thoughts form like software running.
9. The Non-Algorithmic Spark: Value spontaneous intuition and flashes of creative insight that arise from inner silence.
10. The Universal Mind Field: Contemplate individual consciousness as a node in an infinite ocean of universal mind.
11. De-Identifying from Content: Remind yourself: "I am not the data, I am not the feed; I am the space in which it appears."
12. The Source of Intelligence: Direct your attention back to the origin from which intelligence itself arises.
VIII. Sleep, Transitions & Digital Twilight (Dharanas 85–96)
1. Screen-to-Sleep Bridge: Put away devices 30 minutes before sleep and rest in the gradual darkening of mental activity.
2. The Threshold of Sleep: Catch the exact moment consciousness slips from waking rational thought into dream states.
3. The Waking Flash: Capture the instant of pure awareness immediately upon waking, before checking notifications.
4. The Dreamlike Nature of Media: View virtual realities, videos, and feeds as waking dreams flowing through consciousness.
5. Lucid Waking: Move through your tech-infused environment with the light, spacious awareness of being in a dream.
6. The Pause Between Tasks: When finishing one project and starting another, take 30 seconds of absolute stillness.
7. Entering the Digital Realm: State your intention before opening an app or browser, remaining anchored in presence.
8. Exiting the Digital Realm: Close tabs consciously, visualizing pulling your attention back into your physical body.
9. The Midnight Silence: Use nighttime quiet to experience the stillness underlying the global digital network.
10. Dissolving Night Fatigue: Lie down flat, feeling all cognitive strain melt downward into the mattress.
11. The State Between States: Rest awareness in transitions—between waking and sleeping, typing and resting, action and inaction.
12. The Eternal Twilight: Realize that pure consciousness neither wakes nor sleeps, but remains eternally present behind all states.
